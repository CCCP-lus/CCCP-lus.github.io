Google DeepMind CEO Demis Hassabis Calls for U.S.-Led AI Standards Body

  • 来源：CNBC
  • 发布时间：2026年7月14日（Tuesday）
  • 原文链接：https://www.cnbc.com/2026/07/14/google-deepmind-demis-hassabis-us-led-ai-standards-body.html
  • 预计阅读时长：15 分钟

全文摘要

Google DeepMind 首席执行官 Demis Hassabis 公开呼吁美国牵头建立一个 AI 标准机构，以应对通用人工智能（AGI）带来的网络安全、生物安全等国家安全风险。他提议建立一种类似 FINRA 的公私合作监管模式，由联邦政府监督，前沿 AI 实验室在模型发布前自愿提交审查，成熟后转为强制。此举正值中美 AI 竞争加剧、中国企业模型在美国市场快速渗透的背景下，Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等公司也表达了类似诉求。

英文原文

[P1] The chief of Google’s AI division has called for the U.S. to spearhead a standards body that will oversee new AI models and assess national security risks including cybersecurity and biological threats.

[P2] Google DeepMind boss Demis Hassabis, a Nobel laureate, said in an article posted on X on Tuesday that “urgent action” was needed to address risks associated with artificial general intelligence (AGI) — the point at which AI matches or surpasses human intelligence.

[P3] “We’ve already seen the challenges frontier models pose for cybersecurity, and other threats including nuclear and bio risks may soon emerge as capabilities continue to advance,” he said.

[P4] Hassabis proposed a U.S.-led public-private partnership overseen by the federal government as a solution to helping tackle these threats. The White House, the State Department and the Department of Commerce have been approached for comment.

[P5] The comments come a month on from sources telling CNBC that Hassabis, alongside Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei, called for a U.S.-led coalition to shape rules and standards around AI at a G7 meeting with tech leaders and heads of state that included President Donald Trump. OpenAI’s Sam Altman also called for a similar body in an article published by the Financial Times earlier this month.

[P6] Despite growing calls among industry leaders for an AI watchdog, the regulation of leading AI models has increasingly been a point of contention between public and private sectors.

[P7] In recent weeks, Anthropic was locked in negotiations with officials after the Trump administration temporarily imposed export controls over an advanced model. OpenAI also faced restrictions as it was initially requested by the U.S. government to limit the rollout of a new model.

[P8] Hassabis said the U.S. was well positioned to lead in developing an AI framework “given its economic and technical standing.”

[P9] “It could establish a new Standards Body modelled on a federally overseen public-private partnership or self-regulatory organisation, much like the Financial Industry Regulatory Authority (FINRA), with a board that includes independent leading technical experts and open-source representatives,” he added. FINRA regulates brokerage firms and exchange markets in the U.S.

[P10] The proposed body would need “substantial“ funding “in order to attract world-class technical talent and provide the necessary compute resources for large-scale testing,” Hassabis said. Funding would “likely” come from industry, he added.

[P11] Frontier labs would initially voluntarily share models with the body for review up to 30 days before release, before becoming mandatory for deployment in the U.S. market after being shown to be “effective.”

[P12] “Specific agentic AI tests could look for attempts to bypass safety guardrails or signs of deception, and ensure best practices, such as digitally watermarking AI-generated images and generating human-readable output tokens to understand model reasoning,” Hassabis said.

[P13] Calls for greater regulatory oversight come as the race between the U.S. and China to develop and deploy AI models heats up.

[P14] Recent model releases from Chinese companies, including DeepSeek and Z.ai, are seen by many as highly competitive compared to leading frontier systems from the likes of Anthropic and OpenAI, and are gaining traction among U.S. companies as AI costs rise.

[P15] As a result, U.S. lawmakers are currently considering how to curb the growing adoption of Chinese AI models by homegrown companies, which the State Department told CNBC raises “serious concerns.”

背景知识补充

  • Google DeepMind：Google 旗下的人工智能研究实验室，以开发 AlphaGo、AlphaFold 等突破性 AI 系统闻名。

  • FINRA：美国金融业监管局（Financial Industry Regulatory Authority），一家由联邦政府监督的证券行业自律组织。
  • Anthropic：美国 AI 安全公司，由前 OpenAI 员工创立，开发了 Claude 系列大模型，以安全对齐研究著称。
  • OpenAI：美国 AI 研究公司，开发了 ChatGPT 和 GPT 系列模型，由 Sam Altman 领导。
  • DeepSeek：中国 AI 公司，以开源大模型著称，其模型性能被认为与美国顶尖系统高度竞争。
  • Z.ai：中国 AI 公司，近期发布的模型因高性价比在美国市场获得关注。
  • AGI：通用人工智能（Artificial General Intelligence），指在几乎所有认知任务上达到或超越人类水平的 AI 系统。
  • frontier models：前沿模型，指当前能力最强、最先进的大规模 AI 模型，通常需要大量算力和数据训练。
  • agentic AI：具有自主规划和执行任务能力的 AI 系统，能独立设定目标并采取行动，被认为可能带来更高的安全风险。

  • compute resources：算力资源，指用于训练和运行 AI 模型的计算硬件（如 GPU、TPU）及相关基础设施。
  • public-private partnership：公私合作伙伴关系，政府与私营企业合作完成公共目标的模式。
