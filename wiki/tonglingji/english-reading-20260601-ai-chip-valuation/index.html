英文晨读：AI芯片与估值飙升

英文晨读：AI芯片与估值飙升

1. 封面信息

  • 标题: Jensen Huang just put Nvidia’s Blackwell and Vera Rubin sales projections into the $1 trillion stratosphere
  • 来源: TechCrunch
  • 作者: Kirsten Korosec
  • 发布时间: 2026年3月16日
  • 原文链接: https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/16/jensen-just-put-nvidias-blackwell-and-vera-rubin-sales-projections-into-the-1-trillion-stratosphere/
  • 预计阅读时长: 15分钟

2. 全文摘要

英伟达CEO黄仁勋在GTC 2026大会上宣布，公司Blackwell和Vera Rubin芯片的订单总额预计将突破1万亿美元。这一预测反映了AI业务的强劲增长，相比去年5000亿美元的需求翻了一番。Rubin架构相比Blackwell在模型训练上快3.5倍，推理任务快5倍，最高可达50 petaflops性能。英伟达计划在下半年开始大规模生产Rubin芯片。

3. 英文原文

1. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang threw out a lot of numbers — mostly of the technical variety — during his keynote Monday to kick off the company’s annual GTC Conference in San Jose, California.

2. But there was one financial figure that investors surely took notice of: his projection that there will be $1 trillion worth of orders for Nvidia’s Blackwell and Vera Rubin chips, a monetary reflection of a booming AI business.

3. About an hour into his keynote, Huang noted that last year Nvidia saw about $500 billion in demand for its Blackwell and upcoming Rubin chips through 2026.

4. “Now, I don’t know if you guys feel the same way, but $500 billion is an enormous amount of revenue,” he said. “Well, I’m here to tell you that right now where I stand — a few short months after GTC DC, one year after last GTC — right here where I stand, I see through 2027, at least $1 trillion.”

5. The Rubin computing chip architecture, which was first announced in 2024, has been described by Huang as the state of the art in AI hardware that outperforms its Blackwell predecessor.

6. The company said in January, when it officially started production of Rubin, it would operate 3.5x faster than the Blackwell architecture on model-training tasks and 5x faster on inference tasks, reaching as high as 50 petaflops.

7. Nvidia has said it expects to ramp up production in the second half of the year.

5. 长难句解析

原句: “Well, I’m here to tell you that right now where I stand — a few short months after GTC DC, one year after last GTC — right here where I stand, I see through 2027, at least $1 trillion.”

结构拆解:

  • 主句: I'm here to tell you that...
  • 宾语从句: that...I see through 2027, at least $1 trillion
  • 插入语: right now where I stand — a few short months after GTC DC, one year after last GTC — right here where I stand

中文翻译: “好吧，我在这里告诉你们，就我目前所处的位置——在GTC DC会议短短几个月后，距离上次GTC会议一年后——就在我此刻站立的地方，我看到到2027年，至少会有1万亿美元。”

语法知识点: 复杂插入语结构、时间状语从句、宾语从句嵌套

原句: The Rubin computing chip architecture, which was first announced in 2024, has been described by Huang as the state of the art in AI hardware that outperforms its Blackwell predecessor.

结构拆解:

  • 主句: The Rubin computing chip architecture...has been described by Huang as the state of the art in AI hardware
  • 非限制性定语从句: which was first announced in 2024
  • 定语从句: that outperforms its Blackwell predecessor

中文翻译: Rubin计算芯片架构于2024年首次发布，被黄仁勋描述为超越其前身Blackwell的AI硬件尖端技术。

语法知识点: 非限制性定语从句、被动语态、定语从句修饰

原句: The company said in January, when it officially started production of Rubin, it would operate 3.5x faster than the Blackwell architecture on model-training tasks and 5x faster on inference tasks, reaching as high as 50 petaflops.

结构拆解:

  • 主句: The company said...
  • 时间状语从句: when it officially started production of Rubin
  • 宾语从句: it would operate 3.5x faster...and 5x faster...
  • 现在分词短语: reaching as high as 50 petaflops

中文翻译: 该公司在一月份正式启动Rubin生产时表示，该架构在模型训练任务上比Blackwell快3.5倍，在推理任务上快5倍，最高可达50 petaflops。

语法知识点: 时间状语从句、比较级结构、现在分词作结果状语

6. 背景知识补充

  • Nvidia GTC: 英伟达GPU技术大会，是AI和图形计算领域最重要的行业会议之一
  • Blackwell架构: 英伟达于2024年发布的AI芯片架构，以数学家David Blackwell命名
  • Vera Rubin架构: 英伟达下一代AI芯片架构，以天文学家Vera Rubin命名，性能大幅超越Blackwell
  • Petaflops: 每秒千万亿次浮点运算，是衡量超级计算性能的单位
  • AI推理: 指运行已训练好的AI模型进行预测或决策，与模型训练相对
