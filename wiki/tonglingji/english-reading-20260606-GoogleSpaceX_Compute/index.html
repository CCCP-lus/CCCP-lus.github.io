Google Will Pay SpaceX $920M Per Month for Compute

Google Will Pay SpaceX $920M Per Month for Compute

来源：TechCrunch | 作者：Sean O’Kane | 发布时间：2026年6月5日

原文链接：https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/05/google-will-pay-spacex-920m-per-month-for-compute/

预计阅读时长：15 分钟

全文摘要

Google 与 SpaceX 签署了一份重磅算力租赁协议：自 2026 年 10 月起至 2029 年 6 月，Google 将每月向 SpaceX 支付 9.2 亿美元，以获取约 11 万块 NVIDIA GPU 及配套 CPU、内存等计算资源。这是 SpaceX IPO 前夕继 Anthropic 之后达成的第二笔天价算力交易——Anthropic 此前已同意每月支付 12.5 亿美元租用 Colossus 1 数据中心全部算力。Google 表示，此举是为应对其 Gemini Enterprise 和 AI 智能体平台超预期的客户需求而采取的短期”桥接”方案。与此同时，Alphabet 今年资本支出已超 1800 亿美元并仍在攀升，SpaceX 则计划以约 1.75 万亿美元估值进行史上最大规模 IPO。全球 AI 算力争夺战正在进入百亿美元月租时代。

英文原文

¶1 SpaceX has lined up another compute deal ahead of its historic IPO, this time with Google. The company announced the deal in a regulatory filing on Friday.

¶2 Under the terms of the deal, Google will pay SpaceX $920 million per month from October 2026 through June 2029 for access to “approximately 110,000 NVIDIA GPUs, CPUs, memory, and other related components.”

¶3 The deal is similar in length and scope to the one SpaceX announced with Anthropic in late May. As part of that deal, Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion per month through 2029 to rent all the available compute from its Colossus 1 data center near Memphis, Tennessee, that xAI — now part of SpaceX — originally built for its own artificial intelligence efforts.

¶4 Google’s deal appears to be paying for roughly half the amount of compute that Anthropic has access to at Colossus 1. SpaceX didn’t say which specific data center Google would be using. CEO Elon Musk has previously suggested his company would reserve the Colossus 2 data center for xAI.

¶5 Anthropic was significantly limited in its compute capacity prior to its deal with SpaceX, raising usage limits on the same day the deal was announced. Google is in a very different position, with some estimates naming it as the world’s largest single owner of AI compute.

¶6 In a statement, a Google representative described the deal as a result of unexpected demand for its recently launched AI products. “Google Cloud and SpaceX are long-time partners,” Google said in a statement. “This is a short-term, timely agreement to ensure we have bridge capacity to meet surging customer demand for our agent platform, Gemini Enterprise, which has been even higher than we expected.”

¶7 But its parent company Alphabet is on a spending spree. Alphabet has already committed to more than $180 billion in capital expenditures this year and has said it expects that to “significantly increase” in 2027. To help with that, Alphabet recently announced an $80 billion equity sale.

¶8 Also like the Anthropic deal, the agreement with Google includes a cancellation clause. Both SpaceX and Google have the option to terminate the agreement with 90 days’ notice after December 31, 2026. Google’s access to the data center will ramp up “through September at a reduced fee,” according to the filing.

¶9 “If we fail to deliver access to the committed amount of GPUs by September 30, 2026, then following a one-month grace period, Google may immediately terminate the agreement or accept the number of GPUs provided” with a reduction in the monthly fees, it reads.

¶10 SpaceX announced the deal just one week before the company’s stock is expected to start trading on the Nasdaq exchange. Paperwork filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission shows the company is aiming to raise around $75 billion at a valuation of around $1.75 trillion — making it the largest in history.

¶11 Google is a longtime investor in SpaceX. Its stake in Musk’s company is expected to be worth more than $100 billion after the IPO. The companies are also reportedly in talks to try to build orbital data centers — a major component of SpaceX’s future plans post-IPO.

长难句解析

句 1（¶3）

As part of that deal, Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion per month through 2029 to rent all the available compute from its Colossus 1 data center near Memphis, Tennessee, that xAI — now part of SpaceX — originally built for its own artificial intelligence efforts.

结构拆解：

  • 主句：Anthropic agreed to pay SpaceX $1.25 billion per month
  • 状语：As part of that deal
  • 时间状语：through 2029
  • 目的状语：to rent all the available compute from its Colossus 1 data center near Memphis, Tennessee
  • 定语从句修饰 Colossus 1 data center：that xAI originally built for its own artificial intelligence efforts
  • 插入语解释 xAI：now part of SpaceX

中文翻译：作为该协议的一部分，Anthropic 同意在 2029 年前每月向 SpaceX 支付 12.5 亿美元，租用其位于田纳西州孟菲斯附近的 Colossus 1 数据中心全部可用算力——该数据中心最初由 xAI（现已并入 SpaceX）为其自身人工智能项目所建造。

语法知识点：定语从句 + 破折号插入语。that 引导的定语从句修饰先行词 Colossus 1 data center；破折号之间的 now part of SpaceX 为省略了 which is 的非限制性同位插入。

句 2（¶7）

Alphabet has already committed to more than $180 billion in capital expenditures this year and has said it expects that to “significantly increase” in 2027.

结构拆解：

  • 并列谓语：has committed to ... + has said ...
  • 第一个谓语宾语：more than $180 billion in capital expenditures
  • 第二个谓语宾语从句：it expects that to "significantly increase" in 2027
    • 其中 that 指代前文的 capital expenditures
    • to "significantly increase" 为不定式作宾语补足语

中文翻译：Alphabet 今年已承诺投入超过 1800 亿美元的资本支出，并表示预计这一数字将在 2027 年”大幅增长”。

语法知识点：并列谓语结构；代词指代（that 回指 capital expenditures）；直接引语嵌套于宾语从句中。

句 3（¶10）

Paperwork filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission shows the company is aiming to raise around $75 billion at a valuation of around $1.75 trillion — making it the largest in history.

结构拆解：

  • 主语：Paperwork
  • 过去分词短语作后置定语：filed with the Securities and Exchange Commission
  • 谓语：shows
  • 宾语从句（省略了 that）：the company is aiming to raise around $75 billion
  • 状语：at a valuation of around $1.75 trillion
  • 结果状语（现在分词短语）：making it the largest in history

中文翻译：提交给美国证券交易委员会的文件显示，该公司计划以约 1.75 万亿美元的估值融资约 750 亿美元——这将成为历史上规模最大的 IPO。

语法知识点：过去分词后置定语；省略 that 的宾语从句；现在分词短语作结果状语（破折号进一步强调因果关系）。

句 4（¶8）

Both SpaceX and Google have the option to terminate the agreement with 90 days’ notice after December 31, 2026.

结构拆解：

  • 主语：Both SpaceX and Google
  • 谓语：have
  • 宾语：the option
  • 不定式作定语修饰 option：to terminate the agreement
  • 方式状语：with 90 days' notice
  • 时间状语：after December 31, 2026

中文翻译：SpaceX 和 Google 均有权在 2026 年 12 月 31 日之后，提前 90 天通知终止该协议。

语法知识点：with + 名词所有格 + notice 为固定法律/商务表达，表示”提前……天通知”；不定式 to terminate 作名词 option 的后置定语。

背景知识补充

  • SpaceX：埃隆·马斯克创办的航天与通信公司，旗下拥有 Starlink 星链和 xAI 人工智能业务，即将进行史上最大规模 IPO。
  • NVIDIA GPU：NVIDIA 生产的图形处理单元，是当前 AI 大模型训练与推理的核心硬件，全球供不应求。
  • Anthropic：Claude 系列大模型的开发商，由前 OpenAI 员工创立，是 OpenAI 最主要的竞争对手之一。
  • Colossus 数据中心：SpaceX/xAI 在美国田纳西州孟菲斯建造的超大规模 AI 数据中心，Colossus 1 已投运，Colossus 2 在建。
  • xAI：马斯克于 2023 年创办的 AI 公司，开发 Grok 系列模型，已并入 SpaceX。
  • Alphabet：Google 的母公司，旗下还包括 Waymo（自动驾驶）、DeepMind（AI 研究）等。
  • Gemini Enterprise：Google 面向企业客户推出的 AI 智能体平台，整合了 Gemini 系列模型。
  • SEC (Securities and Exchange Commission)：美国证券交易委员会，负责监管证券市场与 IPO 审批。
  • Nasdaq：纳斯达克证券交易所，以科技股为主的美国第二大证券交易所。
  • CapEx (Capital Expenditure)：资本支出，企业用于购买或升级固定资产（如数据中心、服务器）的投入。
  • Orbital Data Center：轨道数据中心，在太空中运行的云计算设施，SpaceX 借助星舰的运载能力提出的未来构想。

第一步：朗读（5 分钟）
先通读英文原文两遍。第一遍不查词典，尝试通过上下文理解大意；第二遍对照生词表，确保每个生词发音准确。注意数字和金额的读法：$920 million = “nine hundred twenty million dollars”；$1.75 trillion = “one point seven five trillion dollars”。

第二步：跟读（5 分钟）
选择 ¶2、¶3、¶6 三个关键信息段落，逐句模仿朗读。重点关注商务英语的正式表达节奏，如 Under the terms of the deal、in a statement、according to the filing。注意 approximately（/əˈprɑːksɪmətli/）、regulatory（/ˈreɡjələtɔːri/）等多音节词的音节划分。

第三步：复述（5 分钟）
合上原文，用英文口头复述三个核心事实：

  1. Google 为何要向 SpaceX 租算力？（surge in demand for Gemini Enterprise）
  2. 这笔交易与 Anthropic 交易有何异同？（half the compute, similar structure with cancellation clause）
  3. SpaceX IPO 的规模有多大？（$75 billion raise, $1.75 trillion valuation）
