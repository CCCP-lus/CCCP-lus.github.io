全文摘要

Nvidia 在德国汉堡举办的 ISC High Performance 2026 大会上宣布将 Agentic AI 引入科学超级计算领域，将其定位为”AI 拐点”——AI 正从单纯回答问题的工具进化为自主执行复杂任务的系统。核心亮点包括：发布 Vera Rubin 机架级平台（单机架 144 GPU、5 PF 双精度浮点、41TB HBM4、3PB/s 带宽）；推出 ALCHEMI、DAQIRI、cuPhoton 三大科学软件栈；Los Alamos 国家实验室将建成全球首批 Agentic AI 超级计算机 Mission、Vision 和 Veritas。大规模供货时间定于 2026 年第四季度，Nvidia 描绘的愿景是人类科学家拥有一支全天候运行的 AI Agent 团队，能够以人类无法企及的规模推进科学发现。

英文原文

[1] At the ISC High Performance 2026 event in Hamburg, Germany, Nvidia laid out its vision for agentic AI in scientific supercomputing, framing it as nothing less than a paradigm shift. The GPU giant argues that AI is no longer merely a question-answering tool — it is becoming an autonomous system capable of executing complex, multi-step scientific workflows without constant human intervention.

[2] “We are currently witnessing a massive inflection point with agentic AI. AI is shifting from a tool that simply answers questions to an autonomous system that executes complex tasks,” said Dion Harris, Nvidia’s senior director of HPC and AI Factory Solutions, during a media briefing. He described a future where scientists leverage agentic AI co-scientists that call simulators, surrogate models, and data analytics tools within a single unified workflow — from hypothesis formation through experiment planning, code writing, simulation execution, and final analysis.

[3] At the hardware heart of this vision sits the Vera Rubin NVL rack, due for availability in Q4 2026. Each rack packs up to 144 GPUs, delivering 5 petaFLOPS of FP64 floating-point performance — the gold standard for scientific precision computing. Recognizing that HPC workloads are frequently memory-bound, Nvidia has equipped each rack with 41 terabytes of HBM4 memory, achieving a staggering three petabytes per second of aggregate bandwidth — a 2.8× improvement over the preceding Blackwell generation.

[4] The most concrete manifestation of Nvidia’s strategy comes from Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL) in the United States, which is building three new supercomputers around the Vera Rubin platform. The flagship Mission system will integrate 2,160 Rubin GPUs alongside 1,080 Vera CPUs; the slightly smaller Vision pairs 1,298 GPUs with 648 CPUs; and the newly announced Veritas deploys 576 Rubin GPUs and 288 Vera CPUs. Together, these systems represent what Harris calls “the world’s first agentic AI supercomputers.”

[5] On the software side, Nvidia unveiled three purpose-built tools for scientific discovery. ALCHEMI is a domain-specific toolkit for chemistry and materials science, using a microservice called BGR to simulate millions of molecules and structures. DAQIRI connects next-generation scientific instruments directly to real-time AI inference — at CERN’s ATLAS experiment, where historically less than 2% of collision data could be stored, DAQIRI introduces a GPU-accelerated AI trigger pipeline that combines low-latency FPGA routing with deep learning models on GPUs to capture significantly more data. cuPhoton is designed to process petabytes of telescope and camera data; in tests with 32 Grace Blackwell superchips simulating data from the Rubin Observatory, cuPhoton loaded and read images 15,000 times faster while accelerating signal processing by up to 8,000 times.

[6] LANL’s URSA (Universal Research and Scientific Agent) framework — a modular, feedback-driven AI system that helps scientists brainstorm hypotheses, plan experiments, run simulations, and analyze results — demonstrated a 7× performance improvement on the Vera CPU compared to the x86 processors in LANL’s current Crossroads supercomputer. A single Vera CPU also outperforms a single-socket x86 CPU by more than 3× while providing over 4× the memory per core.

[7] Beyond LANL, the Leibniz Supercomputing Centre in Germany is building its “Blue Lion” system on Vera Rubin, expected to deliver 30 times the computing power of its predecessor by 2027. Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory is using the platform for its flagship “Doudna” supercomputer, targeting large-scale HPC and AI workloads across high-energy physics and drug discovery.

[8] When asked why agentic AI should be embedded into scientific research — an endeavor fundamentally driven by human curiosity — Harris offered a pragmatic answer: “Agentic AI, or in fact any AI, is not required to do science. But Nvidia believes agentic AI is already emerging as a powerful tool to do science at a scale that isn’t possible when human scientists alone drive the process. Agents don’t need to sleep, or eat, or take breaks. They can consume thousands or millions of technical papers and remember the details, and in some cases, they benefit from PhD-level understanding across diverse fields from astrophysics to zoology.” The vision is clear: human scientists, supported by a tireless team of AI agents running around the clock, will be able to pursue investigations they could never undertake alone.

背景知识补充

  • ISC High Performance：一年一度在德国汉堡举办的国际超级计算大会，是全球 HPC 领域最具影响力的学术与产业盛会之一。
  • Los Alamos National Laboratory (LANL)：美国洛斯阿拉莫斯国家实验室，隶属能源部，以核武器研发和前沿科学研究著称，二战期间”曼哈顿计划”的诞生地。
  • Vera Rubin：薇拉·鲁宾（1928–2016），美国天文学家，通过星系旋转曲线研究为暗物质存在提供了关键证据。Nvidia 以她的名字命名新一代 GPU 架构，致敬其对科学发现的贡献。
  • HBM4：第四代高带宽内存，堆叠式 DRAM 设计，专为 AI 和 HPC 场景提供超高带宽和能效比。
  • FP64：64 位双精度浮点运算格式，科学计算和数值模拟的标准精度要求，相比 AI 训练常用的 FP16/BF16 精度更高但计算成本更大。
  • CERN ATLAS：欧洲核子研究中心大型强子对撞机上的 ATLAS 探测器实验，2012 年参与发现希格斯玻色子，每秒产生海量粒子碰撞数据。
  • Rubin Observatory：薇拉·鲁宾天文台（原名 LSST），位于智利，配备 32 亿像素的全球最大数码相机，计划十年内拍摄整个南天星空以研究暗物质和暗能量。
  • URSA：Universal Research and Scientific Agent，LANL 开发的模块化自主科学 Agent 框架，可帮助科学家进行假设构思、实验规划、模拟运行和结果分析。
  • Leibniz Supercomputing Centre：德国莱布尼茨超级计算中心（LRZ），巴伐利亚科学院下属机构，运营德国顶级超级计算机。
  • Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory：劳伦斯伯克利国家实验室，隶属美国能源部，由加州大学管理，在高能物理、材料科学、生物能源等领域处于国际领先地位。
