全文摘要

Google DeepMind 于 2026 年 7 月 17 日正式发布旗舰模型 Gemini 3.5 Pro，该模型比原计划推迟六周上线，原因是工程师发现原始版本在递归工具调用方面存在结构性缺陷，团队决定放弃修补、转而从头重新预训练。新模型的核心卖点是 200 万 token 的上下文窗口——当前前沿模型的最高水平，用户可在单次请求中传入整本书、完整代码库或全部公司档案。同步推出的 Deep Think 扩展推理模式专为复杂多步任务设计（数学推理、法律分析、长期规划等），但仅限每月 $250 的 Ultra 订阅用户使用。值得注意的是，此次发布恰逢 GPT-5.6 和 Grok 4.5 相继上线，三大前沿模型在两周内密集发布，意味着 AI 竞赛非但未放缓，反而正在加速。

英文原文

Paragraph 1

Google DeepMind launched Gemini 3.5 Pro on 17 July 2026, marking the general availability of its most capable model to date. The model was originally announced at Google I/O in May 2026 with a June target, but the company delayed it by six weeks after engineers discovered structural failures in the original model’s recursive tool-calling behaviour. Rather than patch the existing model, Google DeepMind rebuilt it on an entirely new pretraining run — a decision widely interpreted as prioritising quality over speed amid intensifying competition from OpenAI’s GPT-5.6 and Anthropic’s Claude Sonnet 5.

Paragraph 2

The headline specification is a 2-million-token context window, double anything currently available at the frontier. In practical terms, this allows a single API request to include approximately two million words of text, code, or mixed data. Users can pass entire books, codebases, or research archives in a single session — tasks that previously required manual chunking and summarisation workarounds. For businesses, this means feeding an entire year of customer support tickets, a full set of supplier contracts, or a complete company knowledge base into one query and receiving a coherent, structured answer instantly.

Paragraph 3

The model also introduces Deep Think, an extended reasoning mode designed for complex multi-step tasks including mathematical reasoning, legal analysis, and long-horizon planning. Deep Think is available exclusively to users on the Gemini Ultra subscription tier at $250 per month. Meanwhile, the standard Gemini 3.5 Pro tier is expected to handle the majority of business tasks at substantially lower cost. Leaked pricing information suggested rates near $1.25 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, though Google has not published official figures.

Paragraph 4

Gemini 3.5 Pro is accessible via the public Gemini API and through Vertex AI, Google Cloud’s enterprise AI platform. The Vertex AI route provides private deployment options, data residency controls, and enterprise service level agreements not available on the consumer product — a critical consideration for operators in regulated industries such as legal, finance, and healthcare. Enterprise Workspace customers on Gemini for Workspace plans should expect access through the Vertex AI route initially, with broader Workspace integration following over subsequent weeks.

Paragraph 5

The launch coincides with the opening of the 2026 World Artificial Intelligence Conference in Shanghai, where Chinese President Xi Jinping delivered his first-ever keynote at the event. The convergence underscores that AI competition has become a top-tier strategic priority for both the United States and China. For businesses evaluating their AI strategy, the broader message is clear: three frontier models — GPT-5.6, Grok 4.5, and now Gemini 3.5 Pro — have launched within a fortnight. The market is not settling; it is accelerating.

长难句解析

句 1

原句：
The model was originally announced at Google I/O in May 2026 with a June target, but the company delayed it by six weeks after engineers discovered structural failures in the original model’s recursive tool-calling behaviour.

结构拆解：

  • The model was originally announced — 主句（被动语态），主语 model
  • at Google I/O in May 2026 — 地点/时间状语
  • with a June target — 伴随状语
  • but the company delayed it by six weeks — 并列分句（转折），主语 company，谓语 delayed
  • after engineers discovered structural failures — 时间状语从句，主语 engineers，谓语 discovered
  • in the original model's recursive tool-calling behaviour — 介词短语修饰 failures

中文翻译：
该模型最初于 2026 年 5 月在 Google I/O 大会上发布，原定 6 月上线，但由于工程师发现原始版本在递归工具调用方面存在结构性缺陷，公司将其推迟了六周。

语法知识点：
被动语态（was announced）+ 并列转折句（but）+ 时间状语从句（after）；注意 with a June target 是简洁的伴随状语，等同于 “with a target launch date in June”。

句 2

原句：
In practical terms, this allows a single API request to include approximately two million words of text, code, or mixed data — tasks that previously required manual chunking and summarisation workarounds.

结构拆解：

  • In practical terms — 状语，表”就实际而言”
  • this allows a single API request to include... — 主句，主语 this，谓语 allows，宾语 a single API request，不定式 to include 作宾补
  • approximately two million words of text, code, or mixed data — include 的宾语
  • — tasks that previously required... — 破折号引出同位语，对前述能力做补充说明
  • that previously required manual chunking and summarisation workarounds — 定语从句修饰 tasks

中文翻译：
就实际而言，这意味着单次 API 请求即可包含约两百万词的文本、代码或混合数据——而这些任务此前需要手动分块处理和摘要绕行方案。

语法知识点：
破折号引出同位语结构（tasks = 前述能力）；定语从句（that required…）；workaround 是地道表达，表示”变通方案/绕过限制的方法”。

句 3

原句：

  • The Vertex AI route provides — 主句，主语 The Vertex AI route，谓语 provides
  • private deployment options, data residency controls, and enterprise service level agreements — 三个并列宾语
  • not available on the consumer product — 过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰前述三个宾语
  • — a critical consideration — 破折号引出总结性同位语
  • for operators in regulated industries — 介词短语修饰 consideration
  • such as legal, finance, and healthcare — 举例补充 regulated industries

中文翻译：
Vertex AI 路径提供私有部署选项、数据驻留控制以及企业级服务水平协议——这些在消费者产品上均不可用，对于法律、金融和医疗等受监管行业的运营者而言，这是一个关键考量。

语法知识点：
三项并列宾语 + 过去分词后置定语（not available…）+ 破折号引出总结性同位语；such as 用于列举，其后接名词/名词短语。

  • for both the United States and China — 介词短语修饰 priority

这一交汇凸显出，AI 竞争已成为中美两国的顶级战略优先事项。

underscore = 强调/凸显（高级动词，可替换 emphasize）；that 引导宾语从句作 underscore 的宾语；top-tier = 最高层级的（复合形容词）；both A and B 并列结构。

背景知识补充

  • Google DeepMind：Google 旗下顶尖 AI 研究实验室，2014 年被 Google 收购，曾开发 AlphaGo、AlphaFold 等里程碑式 AI 系统，2023 年与 Google Brain 合并后统一负责 Google 的 AI 模型研发。
  • Gemini（双子座）：Google 的多模态大语言模型系列品牌，对标 OpenAI 的 GPT 系列，2023 年底首次发布，目前最新旗舰为 Gemini 3.5 Pro。
  • Context Window（上下文窗口）：指大语言模型单次能”记住”并处理的 token 总量，是衡量模型能力的关键指标。1 token ≈ 0.75 个英文单词。200 万 token 约等于 150 万英文单词，相当于三本《战争与和平》的篇幅。
  • Vertex AI：Google Cloud 的企业级 AI 平台，提供模型训练、部署、管理的全套工具链，面向对数据安全有严格要求的金融、医疗、法律等受监管行业。
  • GPT-5.6 / Grok 4.5 / Claude Sonnet 5：分别为 OpenAI、xAI（马斯克旗下）、Anthropic 的最新旗舰 AI 模型，与 Gemini 3.5 Pro 构成 2026 年 7 月的前沿模型”四强”格局。
  • Recursive Tool-Calling（递归工具调用）：AI 模型在完成任务时自动多次调用外部工具（如计算器、搜索引擎等）的能力。若该机制设计有缺陷，可能在复杂任务中产生错误循环或逻辑断裂。
  • World AI Conference (WAIC) 世界人工智能大会：自 2018 年起在上海举办的年度 AI 盛会，是中国规格最高的人工智能领域国际会议。
