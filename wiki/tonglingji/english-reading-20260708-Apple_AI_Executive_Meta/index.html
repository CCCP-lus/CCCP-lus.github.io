英文晨读：Apple’s Top AI Executive Jumps to Meta in Record $200M Talent War

标题 Apple’s Top AI Executive Ruoming Pang Defects to Meta in Record $200M Talent Grab
来源 Bloomberg / Reuters / MacRumors
发布时间 2025年7月7日–8日
原文链接 MacRumors / Reuters
预计阅读时长 约 12 分钟

全文摘要

苹果公司基础模型团队（AFM）负责人庞若明（Ruoming Pang）已确认跳槽至 Meta 新成立的超级智能实验室（Meta Superintelligence Labs），Meta 为此开出了总价值超过 2 亿美元的多年薪酬包。庞若明在苹果领导约 100 人的团队，开发支撑 Apple Intelligence 各项功能（邮件摘要、Genmoji、优先通知、新版 Siri）的大语言模型。此次离职是苹果 AI 团队自 Apple Intelligence 项目启动以来最重要的人事变动，副手 Tom Gunter 已于上月先行离职，多名工程师亦在考虑跳槽。这一事件凸显硅谷 AI 人才争夺战已进入白热化阶段——Meta CEO 扎克伯格正以史无前例的高薪从各大公司挖角顶尖 AI 研究者，OpenAI CEO Sam Altman 公开证实 Meta 曾开出高达 1 亿美元的签约奖金。

英文原文

§1 Meta offered one of Apple’s top artificial intelligence executives over $200 million to lure him away from the company, Bloomberg reports. Ruoming Pang, who until recently led Apple’s foundation models team, departed the company to join Meta’s Superintelligence Labs, a newly established division tasked with building advanced AI systems capable of performing at or beyond human-level intelligence.

§2 At Apple, Pang was in charge of a team with approximately 100 employees that work on Apple’s large language models. Models developed by Pang’s team are used for Apple Intelligence features like email summaries, Priority Notifications, and Genmoji. The team’s work also underpins the next-generation Siri, though Apple is reportedly debating whether to pivot to third-party models from OpenAI or Anthropic instead—a decision that has already dampened morale within the AFM team.

§3 Pang joined Apple in 2021 after a 15-year tenure at Google, where he worked as a principal software engineer on large-scale machine learning systems, including speech recognition and privacy-preserving technologies. He holds a Ph.D. in computer science from Princeton University (2006), a master’s from the University of Southern California, and a bachelor’s from Shanghai Jiao Tong University. At Apple, he held the title of Senior Distinguished Engineer—a rarified rank bestowed upon only a handful of the company’s most venerated technologists.

§4 People familiar with the matter told Bloomberg that Meta’s offer to Pang includes a substantial base salary, a signing bonus, and a large stock award that forms the majority of the compensation. The full payout is contingent upon performance milestones and continued employment over several years. Apple apparently did not even attempt to match the offer. The proposed sum significantly exceeds the compensation of all Apple employees, other than that of CEO Tim Cook. Pang’s package is among the highest ever offered in a corporate setting, rivaling compensation for chief executives at major global banks.

§5 The defection is not an isolated incident. Pang’s deputy, Tom Gunter, departed Apple last month, and several other AFM engineers have told colleagues they plan to jump ship soon, with Meta as their most likely destination. In a podcast interview last month, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman shed light on the escalating talent war: “[Meta] started making these giant offers to a lot of people on our team. You know, like $100 million signing bonuses, more than that in compensation per year.”

§6 Apple has reportedly appointed Zhifeng Chen as the new head of its Foundation Models team and implemented a more distributed management structure, with responsibilities split among several senior engineers including Chong Wang, Zirui Wang, Chung-Cheng Chiu, and Guoli Yin. In addition to Pang, Meta’s Superintelligence Labs now includes prominent figures such as former GitHub CEO Nat Friedman, AI startup founder Daniel Gross, and Scale AI co-founder Alexandr Wang, who was named Meta’s chief AI officer after Meta acquired a 49% stake in his company.

§7 The broader picture is clear: Silicon Valley’s AI talent war has reached a fever pitch. According to LinkedIn data, 72% of top-tier AI talent moves in the first half of 2025 were concentrated among just three companies—Meta, OpenAI, and Google. Even as talk of an AI bubble grows louder, leaders like Zuckerberg, Altman, and Anthropic’s Dario Amodei show little sign of restraint, offering compensation packages worth tens or even hundreds of millions of dollars to persuade top researchers that their vision for superintelligence will prevail.

长难句解析

句 1

Ruoming Pang, who until recently led Apple’s foundation models team, departed the company to join Meta’s Superintelligence Labs, a newly established division tasked with building advanced AI systems capable of performing at or beyond human-level intelligence.

结构拆解：

  • 主语：Ruoming Pang
  • 非限制性定语从句修饰主语：who until recently led Apple's foundation models team
  • 谓语：departed
  • 宾语：the company
  • 目的状语：to join Meta's Superintelligence Labs
  • 同位语解释 Superintelligence Labs：a newly established division
  • 后置定语修饰 division：tasked with building advanced AI systems
  • 后置定语修饰 systems：capable of performing at or beyond human-level intelligence

中文翻译：庞若明此前一直领导苹果基础模型团队，现已离职加入 Meta 的超级智能实验室——这是一个新成立的部门，其任务是构建能达到或超越人类智能水平的先进 AI 系统。

语法知识点：

  • 同位语结构 a newly established division 对前一名词进行补充说明
  • tasked with 是过去分词短语作后置定语，相当于 which is tasked with
  • capable of + V-ing 形容词短语作后置定语
  • at or beyond 表示”达到或超越”，是递进式并列

句 2

The full payout is contingent upon performance milestones and continued employment over several years, and Apple apparently did not even attempt to match the offer.

  • 并列句（由 and 连接）
  • 第一分句主语：The full payout
  • 第一分句系动词：is
  • 第一分句表语：contingent upon performance milestones and continued employment over several years
  • 第二分句主语：Apple
  • 第二分句谓语：did not attempt
  • 第二分句状语：apparently，even（强调）
  • 第二分句宾语：to match the offer

中文翻译：这笔薪酬的完全兑现取决于业绩里程碑和持续受聘数年，而苹果公司显然甚至没有尝试匹配这份报价。

语法知识点：

  • be contingent upon = depend on，书面语色彩重
  • did not even attempt to 中 even 加强否定语气，表示”连尝试都没有”
  • match the offer 商务英语常见搭配，意为”匹配对方的出价”

  • 破折号后的同位语：a decision
  • 定语从句修饰 decision：that has already dampened morale within the AFM team

中文翻译：该团队的工作还支撑着下一代 Siri，尽管据报道苹果正在讨论是否转而使用来自 OpenAI 或 Anthropic 的第三方模型——这一决策已经挫伤了 AFM 团队的士气。

  • 破折号后的 a decision 是同位语，概括前面整个从句的内容
