全文摘要

台积电 6 月营收达 NT$4426.8 亿（约 138 亿美元），同比暴增 67.9%，创公司近四十年历史单月最高纪录；Q2 总营收约 $396 亿，超出此前 $390–$402 亿的指引上限。这一增长由 AI 芯片需求驱动，彻底打破了台积电以往 6 月因消费电子淡季而环比下降的季节性规律。然而，N3 制程与 CoWoS 先进封装双双售罄构成双重产能瓶颈——几乎所有 AI 加速器都依赖 TSMC 的这两项独家能力。台积电正以史无前例的规模扩产（2026 年资本支出近 $560 亿），但分析师估计供需缺口到年底才能从 20% 收窄至 10%。周四公布的完整 Q2 财报将提供全年前瞻指引。

英文原文

Paragraph 1

A typhoon that shut down Taiwan’s financial markets last Friday gives context for the timing of what TSMC announced Monday morning: the company’s highest-ever monthly revenue figure, delayed two days by Typhoon Bavi, arrived to confirm that AI-driven chip demand has permanently disrupted the company’s seasonal revenue pattern. In June, TSMC reported consolidated net sales of NT$442.68 billion — approximately $13.8 billion — a 67.9% jump from June 2025 and a 6.2% increase from May 2026. The figure is the largest in the company’s nearly four-decade history. The June figures pushed TSMC’s second-quarter total to approximately NT$1.27 trillion, or about $39.6 billion — edging past the top of guidance management had set in April.

Paragraph 2

For investors tracking TSMC’s quarterly rhythm, the most significant number in Monday’s report is not the year-over-year growth rate — it is the sequential gain. TSMC recorded month-over-month revenue declines in every June for the previous four years, reflecting the traditional summer softening in consumer electronics demand ahead of the second-half product cycle. June 2026’s 6.2% sequential increase breaks that pattern and suggests the AI-driven demand filling TSMC’s order books no longer follows the consumer calendar at all.

Paragraph 3

TSMC’s most advanced production process for AI chips — N3, marketed as “3-nanometer” — operates on booking cycles that run 12 to 18 months ahead of delivery. N3 manufacturing lines are fully committed for all of 2026. The bottleneck extends beyond silicon fabrication: before any AI accelerator can be deployed, the logic die must be integrated with High Bandwidth Memory stacks in a process called CoWoS (Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate), TSMC’s proprietary 2.5D advanced packaging architecture. There is no merchant market for CoWoS — a fabless AI chip designer has no alternative supplier. This creates a double chokepoint: TSMC controls both the logic die fabrication step and the packaging step required to complete an AI accelerator. CEO C.C. Wei described CoWoS capacity as “extremely tight and sold out through 2026.”

Paragraph 4

TSMC’s response to the supply shortfall has been aggressive. Two new advanced packaging plants at the Chiayi Science Park in southern Taiwan are under construction, with the first entering mass production in June. TSMC’s Arizona campus has expanded from an initial two-fab plan to a six-fab commitment, representing the largest foreign direct investment in a greenfield project in U.S. history. The company has indicated it will direct close to $56 billion to capital expenditures in 2026 alone — the largest annual capex in its history. Yet the gap between packaging supply and demand that ran as wide as 20% earlier this year is only expected to narrow to approximately 10% by year-end.

Paragraph 5

There is a downstream consequence that reaches beyond the data center. TSMC has notified major customers of price increases of 5% to 10% across all chipmaking processes at 7nm and below. For hyperscale AI customers like Nvidia, these increases are largely absorbed because AI chip margins are wide. For smartphone and PC chipmakers operating on thinner margins — Qualcomm and MediaTek most prominently — the calculation is different. A meaningful portion of TSMC’s price increases is expected to reach device makers and ultimately consumers. If you are planning to buy a flagship phone or laptop in Q4, TSMC’s pricing power is a direct input into what you will pay.

背景知识补充

  • TSMC（台积电）：全球最大的纯晶圆代工厂，总部位于台湾新竹，市值约 $1.96 万亿，客户包括 Nvidia、Apple、AMD、Qualcomm 等。
  • CoWoS（Chip-on-Wafer-on-Substrate）：台积电专有的 2.5D 先进封装技术，通过硅中介层将逻辑芯片与 HBM 内存堆叠在一起，实现 TB/s 级的数据带宽，是所有高端 AI 加速器的关键封装方案。
  • N3 制程：台积电第三代 3nm 级 FinFET 晶体管工艺节点，”3nm”仅为市场命名而非实际物理尺寸，相比 N5 在同功耗下性能提升约 15-20%。
  • EUV 光刻（Extreme Ultraviolet Lithography）：使用 13.5nm 波长光刻的技术，由荷兰 ASML 独家供应设备，是 7nm 以下先进制程不可替代的生产工具。
  • HBM（High Bandwidth Memory）：高带宽内存，通过 3D 堆叠 DRAM 芯片实现远高于传统 DDR 内存的数据吞吐量，是 AI 训练/推理芯片的核心组件。
  • Nvidia H100 / B200：Nvidia 面向数据中心 AI 的旗舰 GPU，H100 使用 CoWoS-S 封装搭配 6 个 HBM3 堆栈（带宽超 3 TB/s），Blackwell 架构 B200 使用 CoWoS-L 搭配 12 个 HBM3E 堆栈。
  • CHIPS Act（美国芯片法案）：2022 年通过的美国联邦法律，拨款约 $527 亿用于振兴美国本土半导体制造，台积电亚利桑那工厂为该法案最大受益项目之一。
