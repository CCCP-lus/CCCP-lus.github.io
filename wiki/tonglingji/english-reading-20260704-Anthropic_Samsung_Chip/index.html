题材 AI 芯片 / 科技产业

全文摘要

Anthropic 正与三星接洽，探讨合作研发自研 AI 芯片。此举紧随其竞争对手 OpenAI 上周携手博通发布首款推理芯片 “Jalapeño” 之后，标志着 AI 大模型公司正集体从 “购买算力” 转向 “定义算力”。Anthropic 对外回应称，包括谷歌、亚马逊和英伟达芯片在内的多元化硬件架构仍是其计算战略的支柱，并未确认与三星的具体合作细节。当前，英伟达在 AI 芯片领域仍占据无可争议的霸主地位，但 OpenAI、微软、Meta、亚马逊、谷歌等科技巨头纷纷下场自研芯片，一场围绕算力自主权的供应链暗战已然打响。三星作为英伟达的重要合作伙伴，已深度嵌入 AI 产业链，此番若与 Anthropic 联手，将进一步加剧 AI 芯片格局的变数。

英文原文

[1] Back in April, Reuters reported that Anthropic was toying with the idea of producing its own AI chips as a means of responding to chip shortages. Now, it would appear that the company is getting serious about this idea.

[2] On Thursday, The Information reported that Anthropic was in contact with Samsung to explore a collaboration around the pending chip. However, Anthropic hasn’t yet decided what the chip will be used for, how it will fit into the server, or how powerful it will be, according to the report.

[3] When reached for comment, Anthropic told TechCrunch that a diversified hardware stack that includes chips from Google, Amazon, and Nvidia will continue to be pivotal to its compute strategy. On the topic of a potential Samsung partnership, the company said it had nothing further to add.

[4] A number of AI companies have sought to develop custom chips — both as a way to create unique hardware for specific compute tasks and to gain a certain amount of independence from Nvidia, which continues to be the undisputed leader of the chip industry.

[5] Anthropic’s announcement may also be a response to one made last week by its key competitor, OpenAI, which has teamed up with Broadcom to announce its own custom-built inference processor, dubbed “Jalapeño.” OpenAI says that the chip is more efficient, demonstrating better performance-per-watt, than other competitor chips. Amazon and Google both offer custom-built TPUs as part of their cloud offering.

[6] Samsung is already deeply embedded in the AI industry, and acts as a major partner of Nvidia, producing chips that the company needs to train or run its AI models. In turn, Samsung uses Nvidia’s software to manufacture its chips. The duo are working on an AI chip factory in South Korea. Samsung has also discussed partnering with Google on its chip-making efforts.

长难句解析

句 1

原句：
A number of AI companies have sought to develop custom chips — both as a way to create unique hardware for specific compute tasks and to gain a certain amount of independence from Nvidia, which continues to be the undisputed leader of the chip industry.

结构拆解：

  • 主句：A number of AI companies（主语） + have sought to develop（谓语） + custom chips（宾语）
  • 破折号后两个并列目的状语：both as a way to ... and to gain ...
  • which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 Nvidia

中文翻译：
众多 AI 公司已开始寻求开发定制芯片——这既是为了给特定计算任务打造专属硬件，也是为了在一定程度上摆脱对英伟达的依赖，而英伟达至今仍是芯片行业无可争议的霸主。

语法知识点：

  • 破折号用于引出补充解释，替代冒号或括号
  • both ... and ... 并列结构
  • which 非限制性定语从句（去掉也不影响主句完整性）

句 2

原句：
When reached for comment, Anthropic told TechCrunch that a diversified hardware stack that includes chips from Google, Amazon, and Nvidia will continue to be pivotal to its compute strategy.

结构拆解：

  • 时间状语（省略结构）：When (Anthropic was) reached for comment
  • 主句：Anthropic（主语） + told（谓语） + TechCrunch（间接宾语）
  • 宾语从句：a diversified hardware stack ... will continue to be pivotal to its compute strategy
  • 定语从句嵌套（宾语从句内部）：that includes chips from Google, Amazon, and Nvidia 修饰 hardware stack

中文翻译：
当被要求置评时，Anthropic 向 TechCrunch 表示，包含谷歌、亚马逊和英伟达芯片在内的多元化硬件架构，仍将是其计算战略的关键支柱。

语法知识点：

  • When reached for comment 是省略主语和 be 动词的时间状语从句（完整形式为 When it was reached for comment）
  • 宾语从句内嵌定语从句，形成”从句套从句”结构

句 3

原句：
Anthropic’s announcement may also be a response to one made last week by its key competitor, OpenAI, which has teamed up with Broadcom to announce its own custom-built inference processor, dubbed "Jalapeño."

结构拆解：

  • 主句：Anthropic's announcement（主语） + may also be（系动词） + a response to one（表语）
  • made last week by its key competitor, OpenAI 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 one（指代 announcement）
  • which 引导非限制性定语从句修饰 OpenAI
  • dubbed "Jalapeño" 为过去分词短语作后置定语，修饰 inference processor

中文翻译：
Anthropic 的此番动作或许也是对其主要竞争对手 OpenAI 上周发布的公告的回应——OpenAI 携手博通发布了其首款定制推理处理器，代号 “Jalapeño”。

语法知识点：

  • one 作代词指代前文出现过的名词（announcement），避免重复
  • 过去分词短语作后置定语（made last week ... / dubbed "Jalapeño"）
  • 非限制性定语从句提供补充信息，用逗号隔开

句 4

原句：
Samsung is already deeply embedded in the AI industry, and acts as a major partner of Nvidia, producing chips that the company needs to train or run its AI models.

结构拆解：

  • 并列谓语：Samsung + is embedded（谓语1） + and + acts（谓语2）
  • producing chips ... 为现在分词短语作伴随状语
  • that the company needs ... 为定语从句修饰 chips

中文翻译：
三星已深度嵌入 AI 产业，并充当英伟达的重要合作伙伴，为其生产训练或运行 AI 模型所需的芯片。

语法知识点：

  • 并列谓语结构（一个主语带两个谓语动词）
  • 现在分词短语作伴随状语，表示与谓语动作同时发生
  • train or run its AI models 中 run 在此语境下意为”运行（模型推理）”而非”跑步”

背景知识补充

  • Anthropic：美国 AI 安全公司，由前 OpenAI 员工创立，旗下拥有 Claude 系列大语言模型，是 OpenAI 最主要的竞争对手之一。
  • OpenAI：ChatGPT 的开发商，全球估值最高的 AI 公司之一，2026年6月与博通联合发布首款自研推理芯片 “Jalapeño”。
  • Nvidia（英伟达）：全球 GPU 和 AI 芯片霸主，其 H100/B200 系列 GPU 是训练和运行大模型的行业标准硬件，市值一度突破万亿美元。
  • Samsung（三星）：韩国科技巨头，除消费电子外，其半导体部门是全球领先的芯片代工和存储芯片制造商，也是英伟达的重要供应伙伴。
  • Broadcom（博通）：美国半导体公司，专注于定制 ASIC 芯片设计和网络基础设施，是 OpenAI Jalapeño 芯片的制造合作方。
  • ASIC（专用集成电路）：Application-Specific Integrated Circuit，为特定用途（如 AI 推理）定制设计的芯片，相比通用 GPU 在特定任务上能效更高。
  • TPU（张量处理单元）：Tensor Processing Unit，谷歌自研的 AI 加速芯片；亚马逊也有类似产品 Trainium/Inferentia。
  • Inference vs. Training：Inference（推理）指用已训练好的模型处理用户请求（如回答 ChatGPT 问题），Training（训练）指用海量数据教会模型。推理芯片和训练芯片的设计侧重点不同。
