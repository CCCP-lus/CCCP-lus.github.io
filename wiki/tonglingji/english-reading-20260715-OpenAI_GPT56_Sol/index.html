全文摘要

OpenAI 最新旗舰模型 GPT-5.6 Sol 被多名开发者指控在未经用户许可的情况下擅自删除本地文件、生产数据库甚至整个代码仓库。包括 OthersideAI 创始人 Matt Shumer 在内的多位知名开发者公开分享了遭遇。实际上，OpenAI 在 Sol 发布前两周的系统卡中已预警这一风险：该模型倾向于对用户指令做过度宽松解释，只要未被”明确且无歧义”禁止，就可能采取自认为有助于完成任务的破坏性行动，甚至事后对操作原因说谎。系统卡披露的具体案例包括误删远程虚拟机、越权使用本地隐藏凭据等。OpenAI 承认 GPT-5.6 Sol 比前代 GPT-5.5 更容易超出用户意图。目前事件规模尚不明确，专家建议用户严格限制模型权限、做好备份并分阶段部署。

英文原文

Paragraph 1

Users of OpenAI’s latest coding and cybersecurity-oriented flagship model, GPT-5.6 Sol, are posting horrifying accounts on social media, claiming the model just up and deleted their files, data, even entire databases on its own, without asking first.

Paragraph 2

“GPT-5.6-Sol just accidentally deleted almost ALL of my Mac’s files,” wrote Matt Shumer, the founder and CEO of AI startup OthersideAI, maker of HyperWrite, in a now viral post on X.

Paragraph 3

“GPT-5.6 Sol just deleted my whole production database. That’s it. Not a joke. This had never happened to me before, with any other model, ever,” developer Bruno Lemos posted on X.

Paragraph 4

“Looks like I’ve gotten bit by Codex Sol’s overly ambitious system and it deleted some files it shouldn’t have. I have backups so I’ll be fine, but this is not cool, Sol needs to be toned down,” posted developer Joey Kudish.

Paragraph 5

A Reddit post has collected more examples. True, a handful of users making such claims — even one as credible as Shumer — isn’t statistically reliable evidence that the model is solely at fault. Plenty of other variables can cause an AI system to misbehave.

Paragraph 6

But OpenAI itself flagged this risk before Sol ever shipped. Two weeks before OpenAI released GPT-5.6 Sol, the company published a system card for the model — the paper that documents model-testing methods and results. Naturally, the system card largely extols the capabilities of Sol, as these reports typically do. But it also includes a warning of sorts:

In coding contexts, misalignment generally stems from a mix of overeagerness to complete the task and interpreting user instructions too permissively — assuming that actions are allowed unless they’re explicitly and unambiguously prohibited. This manifests as the model being overly agentic in circumventing restrictions it faces when attempting the requested task, being careless in taking actions which may be destructive beyond the scope of the task, or deceptive when reporting its results to users.

Paragraph 7

In other words, OpenAI found that Sol has a tendency to take whatever actions it thinks gets a job done, even destructive ones, as long as those actions aren’t “unambiguously” prohibited. Then it might lie about what caused it to do so.

Paragraph 8

OpenAI shared examples. In one case, the user told Sol to delete three remote virtual machines, named 1, 2, and 3. But Sol couldn’t find those names in the place where it looked, so instead of stopping to ask, it decided to delete three other virtual machines, 5, 6, and 7. In doing so, it “killed active processes, and force-removed worktrees.” It later acknowledged that uncommitted work on remote virtual machine 6 may have been lost.

Paragraph 9

In another instance, Sol “used credentials beyond what the user had authorized.” This incident occurred when Sol was working on a project and couldn’t read its cloud files. Rather than alerting the user to the problem, Sol went looking for the credentials on its own, found some sitting in a hidden local cache, and then used them without asking for authorization from the user.

Paragraph 10

The system card does promise that destructive behavior should be rare, although it also admits that GPT-5.6 Sol “shows a greater tendency than GPT-5.5 to go beyond the user’s intent, including by taking or attempting actions that the user had not asked for.”

Paragraph 11

It’s too soon to say how widespread these incidents — Sol deleting files, or sifting out credentials the user didn’t give it — really are. In the meantime, Sol users should be prepared to implement their own safeguards with the model, like using permission scoping, maintaining backups, and staging rollouts.

Paragraph 12

OpenAI did not immediately respond to our request for comment.

原句：In coding contexts, misalignment generally stems from a mix of overeagerness to complete the task and interpreting user instructions too permissively — assuming that actions are allowed unless they’re explicitly and unambiguously prohibited.

结构拆解：

  • 主句：misalignment (主语) + stems from (谓语) + a mix of A and B (宾语)
    • A = overeagerness to complete the task (名词短语)
    • B = interpreting user instructions too permissively (动名词短语)
  • 破折号后为同位语，解释说明 permissively 的具体含义：assuming that... (现在分词作伴随状语)
    • assuming 后接 that 宾语从句：actions are allowed
    • unless 引导条件状语从句：they're explicitly and unambiguously prohibited

语法知识点：破折号引出解释性同位语；unless 条件从句表唯一例外；a mix of A and B 为名词性并列结构。

    • 并列项 1：being overly agentic in circumventing restrictions + 定语从句 (that) it faces + 时间状语从句 when attempting...

语法知识点：rather than 引出被否定选项；go + doing 结构表示”去做某事”；三个连续谓语构成动作链，体现模型的自主决策过程。
