英文晨读 20260723 Trump AI Action Plan

封面信息

  • 原文链接：https://www.reuters.com/technology/artificial-intelligence/trump-administration-supercharge-ai-sales-allies-loosen-environmental-rules-2026-07-23/

全文摘要

特朗普政府于7月23日正式发布「Winning the Race: America’s AI Action Plan」，这份28页的战略文件包含超过90项政策建议，核心围绕三大支柱：加速创新、建设AI基础设施、主导国际AI外交与安全。同日，特朗普签署三项行政令，分别涉及放松数据中心环境审批、扩大对盟友的「全栈式AI出口包」以及限制联邦采购中存在意识形态偏见的AI模型。这一计划标志着美国AI政策从拜登时代的「高墙」限制模式向以放松管制、快速部署为导向的重大转向。该计划预计将使英伟达、AMD、谷歌、微软、OpenAI、Meta等科技巨头显著受益，同时也引发了环保组织和公民社会对能源消耗与监管缺失的担忧。

英文原文

[1] The Trump administration on Wednesday released a sweeping artificial intelligence blueprint that aims to accelerate U.S. leadership in the sector by loosening environmental regulations and vastly expanding AI exports to allied nations, in a bid to maintain the American edge over China in the critical technology.

[2] President Donald Trump marked the plan’s release with a speech where he laid out the stakes of the technological arms race with China, calling it a fight that will define the 21st century. “America is the country that started the AI race. And as President of the United States, I’m here today to declare that America is going to win it,” Trump said.

[3] The plan, which includes some 90 recommendations, calls for the export of U.S. AI software and hardware abroad as well as a crackdown on state laws deemed too restrictive to let it flourish — a marked departure from predecessor Joe Biden’s “high fence” approach that limited global access to coveted AI chips. “We also have to have a single federal standard, not 50 different states regulating this industry in the future,” Trump said.

[4] Michael Kratsios, head of the Office of Science and Technology Policy, told reporters the departments of Commerce and State will partner with the industry to “deliver secure full-stack AI export packages, including hardware models, software applications and standards to America’s friends and allies around the world.” An expansion in exports of a full suite of AI products could benefit AI chip juggernauts Nvidia and AMD, as well as AI model giants Alphabet’s Google, Microsoft, OpenAI and Facebook parent Meta.

[5] Trump signed three executive orders on Wednesday that incorporated elements of the action plan. The first order loosens environmental rules to fast-track AI data center construction, including exemptions under the National Environmental Policy Act and streamlined permits under the Clean Water Act. The second establishes a framework for chip exports to allied nations. The third seeks to limit political bias in AI technology, requiring that large language models used by the federal government be “ideologically neutral.”

[6] The action plan is structured around three overarching pillars. Pillar One — Accelerating AI Innovation — emphasizes deregulation, open-source development, and reducing federal barriers to AI adoption. It recommends that the Federal Trade Commission review investigations initiated during the Biden administration to ensure they do not unduly burden AI innovation. Pillar Two — Building American AI Infrastructure — focuses on creating more efficient processes for building data centers, semiconductor facilities, and energy infrastructure. Pillar Three — Leading International AI Diplomacy and Security — centers on countering China’s AI ambitions by increasing exports to allies while restricting adversaries’ access to advanced compute capabilities.

[7] The Biden administration had feared U.S. adversaries like China could harness AI chips produced by companies like Nvidia and AMD to supercharge their militaries and harm allies. Biden’s approach imposed strict limits on the sale of advanced semiconductors to China. Trump, by contrast, blocked the export of Nvidia’s H20 chip to China in April but allowed limited sales to resume in July — a move that drew sharp criticism from some Republicans. The new action plan largely sidesteps these national security concerns, prioritizing commercial expansion and global market dominance instead.

[8] The rollout comes as private-sector AI infrastructure investment surges. Earlier this month, companies pledged some $90 billion to build data centers and upgrade energy infrastructure. AMD CEO Lisa Su praised the plan, stating: “For the U.S. to lead in AI, we have to run fast, and the AI action plan is a great way of just laying out all the various pieces that will be helpful for us to run fast.”

[9] Critics, however, warned that the fossil-fuel-heavy approach endangers climate goals, while civil society groups raised concerns about the broader deregulatory posture. The plan recommends that a federal AI-risk-management framework “eliminate references to misinformation, Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion, and climate change” — language that signals the administration’s determination to reshape AI governance along ideological lines as well as economic ones.

背景知识补充

  • AI Action Plan（AI 行动计划）：全称「Winning the Race: America’s AI Action Plan」，是特朗普政府根据 2025 年 1 月签署的第 14179 号行政令（「Removing Barriers to American Leadership in Artificial Intelligence」）在 180 天期限内制定的综合性 AI 国家战略，共 28 页，超过 90 项政策建议。
  • Michael Kratsios：白宫科技政策办公室（OSTP）主任，特朗普政府 AI 政策的核心推手之一，此前曾担任首席技术官。
  • David Sacks：特朗普任命的 AI 与加密资产特别顾问，以批评 AI 模型中的「觉醒文化」偏见而闻名，是此次行动计划中意识形态中立条款的主要推动者。
  • Nvidia H20 芯片：英伟达为遵守美国对华出口管制而设计的降规版 AI 芯片，性能低于 H100/H200，但仍处于管制灰色地带。特朗普在 2026 年 4 月曾禁止其出口，7 月又恢复有限销售。
  • National Environmental Policy Act（NEPA，国家环境政策法）：美国 1970 年颁布的基础性环境法律，要求联邦机构对重大项目进行环境影响评估。此次行动计划提议对 AI 数据中心建设豁免 NEPA 审查以加速项目落地。
  • Clean Water Act（清洁水法）：美国 1972 年颁布的水污染控制法律。数据中心因冷却系统大量用水，通常需要该法下的审批许可。
  • Biden’s “high fence” approach：指拜登政府时期对华 AI 芯片出口采取的严格限制政策，核心是 2023 年 10 月升级的出口管制规则，大幅限制了英伟达等公司向中国出售先进 AI 芯片。
  • Lisa Su（苏姿丰）：AMD 公司 CEO，半导体行业代表人物，在此次计划发布时公开表示支持，认为放松管制将加速美国 AI 技术的采用。
  • FTC（联邦贸易委员会）：美国主要的反垄断和消费者保护执法机构，曾在 2024 年发起「Operation AI Comply」行动打击 AI 虚假宣传。行动计划建议 FTC 审查拜登时期发起的调查是否过度阻碍 AI 创新。
