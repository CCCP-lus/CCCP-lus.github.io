英文晨读 20260603 RTX Spark

封面信息

  • 标题：NVIDIA and Microsoft Reinvent Windows PCs for the Age of Personal AI
  • 来源：NVIDIA Newsroom（nvidianews.nvidia.com） / The Verge
  • 发布时间：2026年6月1日（COMPUTEX 2026 / GTC Taipei）
  • 原文链接：https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/nvidia-microsoft-windows-pcs-agents-rtx-spark/
  • 作者：NVIDIA 官方新闻稿 / The Verge: Sean Hollister
  • 预计阅读时长：15 分钟

全文摘要

NVIDIA 在 COMPUTEX 2026 上正式发布了 RTX Spark 超级芯片，标志着这家 GPU 巨头首次以完整处理器供应商的身份进军消费级 PC 市场。该芯片由英伟达与联发科合作定制，采用台积电 3 纳米制程，集成 20 核 Grace CPU 与 Blackwell RTX GPU（6144 个 CUDA 核心），配备高达 128GB 统一内存，可提供 1 Petaflop 的 AI 算力。RTX Spark 的核心定位是为”个人 AI 智能体”时代打造的 Windows PC 平台，能够在本地运行 1200 亿参数大语言模型。英伟达与微软深度合作，推出了基于 Windows 安全基元和 NVIDIA OpenShell 运行时的设备端智能体安全方案。华硕、戴尔、惠普、联想、微软 Surface 及微星将于今年秋季推出首批搭载 RTX Spark 的轻薄笔记本和紧凑型台式机，Adobe 也宣布从底层重构 Photoshop 和 Premiere 以适配新平台。黄仁勋将此变革类比为”功能手机向智能手机的跨越”，宣称 PC 正在被重新定义——用户只需提出需求，PC 自动完成工作。

英文原文

Paragraph 1

NVIDIA today unveiled NVIDIA RTX Spark™, a new superchip that reinvents Windows PCs for the era of personal AI agents — offering a new class of computer that moves from tool to teammate. Designed for AI, creating and gaming, RTX Spark brings together 30 years of NVIDIA innovation — including NVIDIA CUDA®, NVIDIA RTX™, DLSS, FP4, NVIDIA TensorRT™, NVIDIA OptiX™, Reflex and G-SYNC® — to slim Windows laptops with all-day battery life and small, ultraefficient desktop PCs.

Paragraph 2

“The PC is being reinvented,” said Jensen Huang, founder and CEO of NVIDIA. “For forty years, you launched apps. Click. Type. With RTX Spark and Microsoft Windows, you ask — and the PC does the work. RTX Spark brings everything NVIDIA has built — CUDA, RTX, our AI platform — into a single superchip. Local agents. Frontier models. Creative workflows. RTX games. All on a laptop. This is the new PC. The personal AI computer.”

Paragraph 3

The RTX Spark superchip features an NVIDIA Blackwell RTX GPU with 6,144 CUDA cores and fifth-generation Tensor Cores with FP4 precision, connected via the NVIDIA NVLink®-C2C chip-to-chip interconnect to a high-performance, 20-core NVIDIA Grace™ CPU. MediaTek, a market leader in Arm-based system-on-a-chip designs, collaborated with NVIDIA on the custom CPU design, contributing to its best-in-class power efficiency, performance and connectivity.

Paragraph 4

AI agents have reached an inflection point, with open source projects such as OpenClaw and Hermes Agent achieving record-breaking numbers on developer networks like GitHub and OpenRouter. Yet broad adoption has been limited by the inability to run agents securely and privately on users’ primary PCs. NVIDIA and Microsoft are partnering to address this challenge by delivering a robust, secure Windows platform for on-device agents. The collaboration begins with a strong foundation — new Windows security primitives and the NVIDIA OpenShell™ runtime — to ensure agents run safely and under full user control.

Paragraph 5

Powering agents on local devices requires both robust security and performant hardware. RTX Spark features up to 1 petaflop of AI compute and 128GB of unified memory to meet the processing demands of on-device agents. “Our goal is to deliver unmetered intelligence to every home and every desk with Windows,” said Satya Nadella, chairman and CEO of Microsoft. “RTX Spark marks a real breakthrough towards that vision.”

Paragraph 6

Users can render ultralarge 90GB 3D scenes with OptiX and DLSS, edit 12K 4:2:2 video with the NVIDIA Blackwell decoder, run 120-billion-parameter large language models with 1 million tokens context, and play AAA games at 1440p resolution and over 100 frames per second. Adobe is rearchitecting Photoshop and Premiere from the ground up for RTX Spark to deliver up to 2x faster AI and graphics performance. RTX Spark-powered slim Windows laptops with all-day battery life and premium displays, as well as compact desktop PCs, will be available this fall from ASUS, Dell, HP, Lenovo, Microsoft Surface and MSI, with models from Acer and GIGABYTE to follow.

长难句解析

句 1

Designed for AI, creating and gaming, RTX Spark brings together 30 years of NVIDIA innovation — including NVIDIA CUDA, NVIDIA RTX, DLSS, FP4, NVIDIA TensorRT, NVIDIA OptiX, Reflex and G-SYNC — to slim Windows laptops with all-day battery life and small, ultraefficient desktop PCs.

  • 结构拆解：

    • Designed for AI, creating and gaming → 过去分词短语作状语，修饰主语 RTX Spark
    • RTX Spark → 主语
    • brings together → 谓语（动词短语）
    • 30 years of NVIDIA innovation → 宾语
    • — including ... G-SYNC — → 插入语，列举创新的具体内容
    • to slim Windows laptops ... and small, ultraefficient desktop PCs → 目的状语

  • 中文翻译：RTX Spark 专为 AI、创作和游戏而设计，将 NVIDIA 三十年的技术创新——包括 NVIDIA CUDA、NVIDIA RTX、DLSS、FP4、NVIDIA TensorRT、NVIDIA OptiX、Reflex 和 G-SYNC——带入拥有全天电池续航的轻薄 Windows 笔记本和小巧超高能效的桌面 PC。

  • 语法知识点：

    • 过去分词作状语（Designed for…）
    • 破折号插入语的使用
    • 多个并列结构（A, B and C）

句 2

Yet broad adoption has been limited by the inability to run agents securely and privately on users’ primary PCs.

  • 结构拆解：

    • broad adoption → 主语（名词短语）
    • has been limited → 谓语（现在完成时被动语态）
    • by the inability → 介词短语作状语，引出原因
    • to run agents securely and privately on users' primary PCs → 不定式短语修饰 inability，其中 securely and privately 为并列副词修饰 run

  • 中文翻译：然而，由于无法在用户的主力 PC 上安全、私密地运行智能体，其广泛普及一直受限。

  • 语法知识点：

    • 现在完成时被动语态（has been limited）
    • “the inability to do sth.” 结构
    • 副词并列修饰（securely and privately）

句 3

The collaboration begins with a strong foundation — new Windows security primitives and the NVIDIA OpenShell™ runtime — to ensure agents run safely and under full user control.

  • 结构拆解：

    • The collaboration → 主语
    • begins with → 谓语（动词短语）
    • a strong foundation → 宾语
    • — new Windows security primitives and the NVIDIA OpenShell™ runtime — → 插入语，解释 foundation 的具体内容
    • to ensure ... → 目的状语从句
    • agents run safely and under full user control → ensure 的宾语从句（省略 that），safely 和 under full user control 并列作状语

  • 中文翻译：合作从一个坚实的基础开始——新的 Windows 安全基元和 NVIDIA OpenShell 运行时——以确保智能体能安全运行，并完全处于用户掌控之下。

  • 语法知识点：

    • ensure 后省略 that 的宾语从句
    • 破折号同位语用法
    • “under + n.” 作状语

句 4

Users can render ultralarge 90GB 3D scenes with OptiX and DLSS, edit 12K 4:2:2 video with the NVIDIA Blackwell decoder, run 120-billion-parameter large language models with 1 million tokens context, and play AAA games at 1440p resolution and over 100 frames per second.

  • 结构拆解：

    • Users → 主语
    • can render ... , edit ... , run ... , and play ... → 并列谓语结构（四个并列动词短语）
    • 每个动词带各自宾语和修饰成分：
      • render ultralarge 90GB 3D scenes + with OptiX and DLSS（工具状语）
      • edit 12K 4:2:2 video + with the NVIDIA Blackwell decoder
      • run 120-billion-parameter large language models + with 1 million tokens context
      • play AAA games + at 1440p resolution and over 100 frames per second

  • 中文翻译：用户可以借助 OptiX 和 DLSS 渲染超过 90GB 的超大 3D 场景，通过 NVIDIA Blackwell 解码器编辑 12K 4:2:2 视频，利用 100 万 token 上下文运行 1200 亿参数大语言模型，并以 1440p 分辨率和超过 100 帧每秒畅玩 AAA 游戏。

  • 语法知识点：

    • 长并列结构（四个并列谓语的排比）
    • 复合形容词（120-billion-parameter）
    • “with + n.” 表示手段/工具

句 5

This robust security and privacy layer is being adopted by leading agent developers such as Hermes Agent and OpenClaw in their new Windows apps, which will make it easy and secure for users to access powerful on-device agents that can execute tasks in Windows applications, reason through cross-app workflows, generate images and video, code plug-ins and apps, and semantically search local files.

  • 结构拆解：

    • This robust security and privacy layer → 主语
    • is being adopted → 谓语（现在进行时被动语态）
    • by leading agent developers such as ... → 介词短语引出动作执行者
    • in their new Windows apps → 地点状语
    • , which will make it easy and secure ... → 非限制性定语从句，修饰 new Windows apps
    • for users to access powerful on-device agents → 不定式的逻辑主语和动作
    • that can execute ... , reason ... , generate ... , code ... , and semantically search ... → 定语从句修饰 agents，内部为五个并列谓语

  • 中文翻译：这一强大的安全与隐私层正被 Hermes Agent 和 OpenClaw 等领先智能体开发者纳入其新款 Windows 应用中，这些应用将使用户能够方便、安全地访问强大的设备端智能体——它们可以在 Windows 应用中执行任务、通过跨应用工作流进行推理、生成图像和视频、编写插件和应用代码，以及对本地文件进行语义搜索。

  • 语法知识点：

    • 现在进行时被动语态（is being adopted）
    • 非限制性定语从句（which 引导）
    • 多层从句嵌套（which → that → 并列谓语）
    • “make it + adj. + for sb. to do” 结构

背景知识补充

  • NVIDIA（英伟达）：全球最大的 GPU 和 AI 芯片公司，市值超 5 万亿美元，此前主要专注于数据中心 AI 芯片和独立显卡市场。
  • Jensen Huang（黄仁勋）：NVIDIA 创始人兼 CEO，被称为”AI 教父”，以标志性黑色皮衣和富有感染力的演讲风格著称。
  • COMPUTEX（台北国际电脑展）：全球最大的 PC 产业展会之一，每年在台北举办，是各大芯片和 PC 厂商发布年度重磅产品的首选舞台。
  • Blackwell 架构：NVIDIA 最新一代 GPU 架构，以数学家 David Blackwell 命名，于 2024 年首次发布，标志着 AI 计算进入全新代际。
  • MediaTek（联发科）：全球领先的 Arm 架构 SoC 设计公司，总部位于台湾，长期主导智能手机芯片市场，此次为 NVIDIA 定制 CPU。
  • Grace CPU：NVIDIA 自研的基于 Arm 架构的高性能 CPU，以计算机先驱 Grace Hopper 命名，首次应用于数据中心，此次首次进入消费级 PC 领域。
  • CUDA：NVIDIA 推出的并行计算平台和编程模型，是 AI 开发者生态的核心基石。
  • Satya Nadella（萨提亚·纳德拉）：微软董事长兼 CEO，自 2014 年执掌微软以来推动了云优先和 AI 优先战略。
  • OpenShell：NVIDIA 推出的 Windows 运行时，用于安全管理设备端 AI 智能体的权限和隐私策略。
  • FP4 精度：4 位浮点数精度，是当前 AI 推理中最低的精度格式之一，可在保持可接受精度的同时大幅提升算力效率。
  • DGX Spark：NVIDIA 此前发布的”个人 AI 超级计算机”迷你 PC，RTX Spark 芯片即基于此产品的 GB10 芯片扩展而来。

晨读建议

第一步：朗读（5 分钟）

  • 先通读全文，标记不熟悉的单词和句子
  • 大声朗读英文原文两遍，第一遍注意发音准确性，第二遍注意语调和节奏
  • 重点练习长难句 4（并列谓语排比句），感受英文科技报道的节奏感

第二步：跟读（5 分钟）

  • 逐段对照中文翻译，确保理解每个句子的含义
  • 针对生词表，用每个单词口头造句，强化记忆
  • 重读长难句解析部分，遮住中文翻译，尝试独立拆解句子结构

第三步：复述（5 分钟）

  • 不借助原文，用英文口头复述新闻核心内容（可从以下要点入手）：
    • What is RTX Spark and why is it significant?
    • What are the key technical specifications?
    • How are NVIDIA and Microsoft collaborating?
    • What does this mean for the PC industry?
  • 复述后对照原文检查遗漏点，补充修正
