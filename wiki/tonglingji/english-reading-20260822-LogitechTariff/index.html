二、全文摘要（中文）

罗技（Logitech）因未把关税退款返还给消费者，正面临一起拟议的集体诉讼。2025 年 4 月，罗技以关税为由对其约 51% 的产品组合提价，部分产品涨幅高达 25%；而 2026 年 2 月美国最高法院裁定特朗普依据《国际紧急经济权力法》（IEEPA）征收的关税非法，罗技随后已从联邦政府获得 6100 万美元退款，却未向消费者退还相应款项。原告加州建筑公司 SJK Development 与居民 Ala Awadalla 在北加州联邦地区法院提起诉讼，指控罗技”双重获利”（先以关税为名多收费，再保留政府退款），并要求法院作出宣告性判决，责令罗技将关税退款收益移交消费者。罗技并非个例：微软、任天堂、索尼等消费电子公司也因类似问题被消费者起诉；任天堂已辩称顾客”得到了所购之物”并申请强制仲裁，罗技也可能效仿这一应诉策略。

三、英文原文（English Original）

1. Logitech is facing a proposed class action lawsuit for not giving customers tariff refunds.

2. In April 2025, Logitech raised the prices on 51 percent of its portfolio, with some prices increasing by as much as 25 percent.

3. The complaint seeks to “recover monies that Logitech extracted from American consumers as offset for import tariffs that the United States Supreme Court has since declared unlawful, tariffs for which Logitech now stands to be reimbursed by the federal government. This is all while Logitech retained every dollar of the corresponding price increases it charged its customers due to the illegal tariffs.”

4. A construction and residential housing builder in California, SJK Development, and California resident Ala Awadalla filed the complaint in the US District Court for the Northern District of California, San Jose Division on Tuesday. Per the complaint, both parties bought Logitech mice at higher prices.

5. “Had Logitech not inflated its prices in the name of the unlawful IEEPA [International Emergency Economic Powers Act] tariffs, Plaintiff[s] would have paid less for the same product. Plaintiff[s] has received no refund, credit, or other compensation from Logitech corresponding to the tariff component of the price Plaintiff[s] paid,” the complaint reads.

6. Logitech didn’t make a public announcement about the price hikes, which are still implemented. The complaint highlights multiple times that Logitech executives told investors that the price increases would or had offset the financial impact of the tariffs. This includes a call in May when Matteo Anversa, Logitech’s CFO, said, “The positive impact of the US price actions and favorable foreign exchange more than offset the impact of tariffs and higher promotions.” Anversa was discussing Logitech’s fiscal Q4 2026, which he noted was “the highest level of profitability in the history of the company outside of the COVID peak.”

7. In February, the Supreme Court ruled that President Donald Trump imposed the IEEPA tariffs illegally. The refund process is ongoing.

8. The complaint notes that “Logitech received a full refund of $61 million for the tariffs invalidated by the Supreme Court, including $15 million during the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 and $46 million subsequent to quarter end.” It adds:

9. … Logitech has been—and absent relief will continue to be—enriched twice over at its customers’ expense: once by the tariff-justified overcharges collected from Plaintiffs and the Class, and again by the government’s refund, with interest, of the very duties those overcharges were represented to cover.

10. The lawsuit is seeking a declaratory judgement that Logitech must give IEEPA tariff refund proceeds to customers.

11. (Tariff lawsuits)

12. Logitech joins a handful of other consumer tech companies, including Microsoft, Nintendo, and Sony, that customers are suing over tariff refunds. Some customers feel entitled to being refunded as these companies were, although mass refunds like this could present financial and logistical challenges.

13. For its part, Nintendo has argued that customers “received exactly what they paid for” and that there is not “anything ‘unjust’ about Nintendo retaining money that it may receive from the government as tariff refunds.” It also filed a motion to compel arbitration, a move that Logitech may also pursue, considering that its end-user license agreement has arbitration provisions.

14. Logitech didn’t respond to Ars Technica’s request for comment.

五、长难句解析

1. 原句： The complaint seeks to “recover monies that Logitech extracted from American consumers as offset for import tariffs that the United States Supreme Court has since declared unlawful, tariffs for which Logitech now stands to be reimbursed by the federal government.”

  • 结构拆解：
    • 主句：The complaint seeks to recover monies
    • 定语从句①：that Logitech extracted from American consumers（修饰 monies）
    • 目的/方式状语：as offset for import tariffs
    • 定语从句②：that the United States Supreme Court has since declared unlawful（修饰 tariffs）
    • 非限制性定语从句：for which Logitech now stands to be reimbursed by the federal government（继续修饰 tariffs）
  • 中文翻译：起诉状要求追回罗技从美国消费者那里收取的钱款——这些钱被当作进口关税的抵偿，而美国最高法院后来已裁定这些关税非法；对于这些关税，罗技现在正等着联邦政府退款。
  • 语法知识点：多个定语从句层层嵌套修饰名词；stand to do 表示”即将/有望做某事”；as offset for 为介词短语作方式状语。

2. 原句： “Had Logitech not inflated its prices in the name of the unlawful IEEPA tariffs, Plaintiff[s] would have paid less for the same product.”

  • 结构拆解：
    • 虚拟条件句（倒装）：Had Logitech not inflated its prices in the name of ...（= If Logitech had not inflated…）
    • 主句：Plaintiff[s] would have paid less for the same product
  • 中文翻译：如果罗技没有打着非法的 IEEPA 关税旗号抬高价格，原告本可以为同样的产品少付一些钱。
  • 语法知识点：与过去事实相反的虚拟语气；条件句省略 if 后 had 提前形成倒装；in the name of 意为”以……的名义”。

    • 定语从句：when Matteo Anversa ... said, ...（when 修饰时间名词 call，相当于 during which）
    • 同位语：Logitech's CFO（解释 Matteo Anversa 身份）
    • 直接引语："The positive impact ... more than offset the impact ..."
  • 中文翻译：其中包括五月的一次电话会，罗技首席财务官 Matteo Anversa 在会上表示：”美国提价行动和有利的外汇走势带来的正面影响，远远超过了关税和更高促销成本带来的影响。”
  • 语法知识点：when 引导定语从句修饰时间名词；同位语补充身份；more than 此处表示”远远超过”，起强调作用。

4. 原句： … Logitech has been—and absent relief will continue to be—enriched twice over at its customers’ expense: once by the tariff-justified overcharges collected from Plaintiffs and the Class, and again by the government’s refund, with interest, of the very duties those overcharges were represented to cover.

六、背景知识补充

  • Logitech（罗技）：瑞士-美国跨国电脑外设制造商，产品涵盖鼠标、键盘、摄像头等，是全球消费电子市场的主要玩家。
  • IEEPA（International Emergency Economic Powers Act）：《国际紧急经济权力法》，美国 1977 年联邦法律，授权总统在宣布国家紧急状态后管制对外经济交易；2026 年 2 月美国最高法院裁定其不能作为总统加征关税的法律依据。
  • class action（集体诉讼）：由一名或多名原告代表”类别”（Class）中处境相同的所有消费者提起的诉讼，法院判决对全体成员具有约束力。
  • US Supreme Court（美国最高法院）：美国联邦最高司法机构，其 2026 年 2 月裁决认定特朗普以 IEEPA 为名对多国商品加征的关税违法，由此引发企业关税退款如何处置的大范围法律争议。
  • motion to compel arbitration（强制仲裁动议）：一方请求法院强制双方按其用户协议中的仲裁条款解决争议，从而绕开集体诉讼程序，是科技公司常见的应诉策略。
